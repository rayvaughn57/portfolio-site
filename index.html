Method

Knowledge Depth Inversion

Most documentation describes the surface. KDI starts from the other end: find what carries the load, then write that down first.

01

Strip the surface

Remove surface complexity until the invariant structure and the load-bearing mechanisms are exposed.

02

Trace across domains

Follow the same pattern through mainframes, networks, databases, logic and search, and note where it holds.

03

Formalize the tacit

Turn what experienced engineers know without saying into portfolio artifacts someone else can use.
